NeuroForge — Intelligent Document Assistant

Sub-track 1C: Searching the Way a Human Does

An agentic, multi-step retrieval assistant that reasons over a 415-document, ~1,277-page fictional archive the way a human researcher would: search, read, decide what's missing, search again, and only then answer — rather than a single-pass lookup. Built to plan and re-plan its retrieval strategy, flag incomplete or conflicting evidence, and trace every claim in its final answer back to a source document.

🛠️
My contribution System integration, documentation, and submission — connecting the retrieval, reasoning, and evaluation pieces into one working pipeline and preparing the team's final report.
